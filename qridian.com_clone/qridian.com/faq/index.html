FORM 9-A: THINGS YOU WERE GOING TO ASK ANYWAY

A pre-emptive intelligence briefing for prospective operatives, curious bystanders, and people who clicked the wrong link.

1.0 REGISTRATION & TIMING

Do we need to book in advance?

Yes. The mystery waits for no one, and we need time to calibrate the clues, print your briefing, and warn the local wildlife you are coming.

When should we arrive for the briefing?

Please arrive 30 minutes before your ticketed start time. This window allows you to complete the event registration, claim your badge of passage, and engage in awkward small talk with your rivals before the clock starts ticking. Arriving early ensures you catch the full safety briefing and ensures your tech is ready before the mystery begins.

How long does it take?

Most teams finish in 1.5 to 3 hours, depending on pace, clue-solving ability, and how often they stop to argue about whether that tree looks “suspicious.”

2.0 PRE-DEPARTURE REQUIREMENTS

What equipment do I need for the event?

Ensure your field unit has provisioned the following items prior to clocking in:

  • A Smartphone: Fully charged with internet access (Essential).
  • Power Bank: Bring backup juice if your phone always seems to go flat.
  • One Adult (18+): To supervise the chaos.
  • Sensible Shoes: It’s a park, not a catwalk.
  • Bribes for the Marshals: (Biscuits preferred, cash accepted).

What if it rains?

Then it’s atmospheric. The QR codes are waterproof; we hope you are too. Bring a coat, a sense of humour, and possibly a laminated friend.

What if we get lost?

You’re not lost. You’re "exploring with intent." (But also, yes, marshals will be on hand to direct you back on track).

3.0 PROTOCOLS & CONDUCT

Do I need to follow a set trail?

No. You’ll be given a physical map and clues, but how you tackle them is up to you. Follow the suggested trail, go rogue, or wander artistically. Just don’t trespass or attempt shortcuts through hedges. We’ve tried. It ends in nettles.

Is this outdoor activity suitable for children and families?

Yes. We design our outdoor adventures to be family-friendly, although the logic puzzles are calibrated for adult brains. However, we find that children are often much better at the physical scavenger hunt elements (spotting the hidden markers) than their parents. We recommend ages 8+, provided they bring a responsible adult to hold the map and stop them climbing the clues.

Is it competitive or just for fun?

Both. Some teams sprint to solve the mystery first to claim intellectual superiority. Others wander slowly, argue over clues, and stop for snacks. All approaches are valid, provided you finish before the park closes.

Is it a race?

Officially? No. It is a cerebral journey of discovery. Unofficially? We know you will check your watch the second you start. (See also column left: Is it competitive?)

4.1 EVENT MANAGEMENT & OVERVIEW

Can I check on an event while it's actually running?

Yes; live status any time you like. Who's playing, how far they've gotten, whether anything's stuck.

What stops a broken event from going live?

The tool checks for missing or broken story pieces before you publish; nothing goes live with holes in it.

Can I archive old events without breaking anything still running?

Yes; building, publishing, and archiving stay out of each other's way.

4.2 NARRATIVE & STORY BLUEPRINTING

Can different teams get different paths through the same story?

Yes; branching paths generate automatically for different teams without building each by hand.

If a scene affects multiple characters, do I write it more than once?

No; write it once and it stays in sync everywhere it appears, no matter how many storylines cross through it.

Is there a proper space for actually writing the story, or just a form?

A dedicated writing space, with just enough surrounding context to stay consistent; nothing gets lost mid-session either.

4.3 CHARACTER & PHYSICAL MEDIA PRODUCTION

Do characters have to be rebuilt for every new event?

No; build a character once and reuse it across your whole catalogue.

Do I need to design QR codes myself?

No; every character's QR code is generated ready to print, one at a time or as a full batch.

4.4 ECONOMY & GAME BALANCE

If my event has trading or crafting, do I control how it works?

Yes; what's tradeable and how it's made is entirely up to you.

How do you make sure the economy actually holds up, no shortcuts, no bottlenecks?

It's tested before launch, so problems like one player cornering the market or trades drying up get caught before your players ever see them. This is what sits behind the Economy Layer you'll see referenced on pricing.

4.5 MAP DESIGN & PRINT RENDERING

Are checkpoints placed on a real map, or just roughly sketched?

A real, accurate map of your actual site; not an approximation.

Can I use a custom illustrated map instead of the standard one?

Yes; export an SVG trace outline of your checkpoints and hand it to your own illustrator, then drop their finished artwork straight back in. This is what the Custom Stylised Map option is.
